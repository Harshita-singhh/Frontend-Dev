Designing for Accessibility

By Harshita — Oct 2025

Accessibility is a critical part of web design. Inclusive design ensures everyone can use the web.

Start with semantic HTML and clear headings to make content easier to navigate.

Use sufficient color contrast and provide keyboard access to interactive elements.

Test with screen readers and real users, iterating to remove barriers.

Document accessibility patterns and include them in the design system.

Designing with accessibility in mind improves the experience for all users.
